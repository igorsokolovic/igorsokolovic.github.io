Hi, 

welcome and thanks for stopping by!

I created this website to share some of my scientific achievements, research experiences, and ideas, but also to make the science behind them more accessible to a wider audience. 

I'm a physicist with a background in both theoretical and experimental condensed matter physics. I studied theoretical condensed matter physics in Novi Sad, before moving to TU Wien for my PhD in experimental surface physics. Much of my research has focused on understanding materials at the atomic scale, particularly their surfaces, where even a single missing atom can make a surprising difference. I enjoy combining experiments with theoretical calculations, trying to understand not just what happens, but also why.

More recently, I've also been exploring microelectronics, with the aim of contributing to better electronic hardware by tackling some of its challenges from a fundamental physics perspective.

In the Blog, which I update periodically, I share some of the stories behind my research. You'll find explanations written for the general public alongside more detailed discussions for scientists, as well as educational posts and occasional personal milestones. I hope these give you a glimpse of what actually goes into scientific research, including the small details that rarely find their way into publications.

I'm always happy to exchange ideas, discuss science, or answer questions. If you'd like to get in touch, you can find my email address in publications where I'm listed as a corresponding author. (hint: look in my 2025 *Nature Communications* paper)
