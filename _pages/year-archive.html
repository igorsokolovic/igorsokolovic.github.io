Welcome!

Here I share my research, explain interesting scientific ideas, and occasionally write about personal milestones and everyday life in science.

Some posts look back at earlier publications and achievements. I'm gradually building a record of my research, with the aim of sharing more frequent updates and glimpses of what happens behind the scenes.

I use AI tools to help draft and edit posts, saving time while keeping the scientific content and responsibility my own. I'm aware and mindful of AI's environmental footprint, and one of my research goals is to help reduce it by developing more energy-efficient electronic components, bringing together fundamental physics and practical device development.

I hope you'll find some enjoyable reads!
