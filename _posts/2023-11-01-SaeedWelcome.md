## Saeed Rasouli Joins the TU-D Doctoral School!

*November 2023 | A new PhD journey begins*

With the start of a new funding period of the **TU-D Doctoral School**, we are also welcoming a new generation of PhD students!

The first to join is **Saeed Rasouli**, whom I will have the pleasure of **co-supervising throughout his PhD research**. Saeed will be working on the project *"Probing and manipulating atomic-scale defects on surfaces of 2D materials"*, investigating how individual defects in two-dimensional materials can be studied and manipulated using advanced scanning probe microscopy techniques.

The research will focus on **noncontact atomic force microscopy (nc-AFM)**, with materials such as PtSe<sub>2</sub> providing an interesting platform for exploring atomic-scale defects and their properties.

I'm looking forward to working together, exchanging ideas, and seeing where the research takes us.

**Welcome, Saeed, and best of luck with your PhD!**
