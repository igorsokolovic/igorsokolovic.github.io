MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships – Seal of Excellence

February 2026 | European Union
I was pleased to receive the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Seal of Excellence for my postdoctoral fellowship proposal submitted in 2025. Although the project did not receive funding, it was evaluated very highly by the European Commission and recognized for its scientific quality.

The 2025 call was particularly competitive, attracting a record 17,066 applications, an increase of nearly 65% compared to 2024, which had itself been a record year. With only around 9.6% of proposals receiving funding, the competition was exceptionally tough.

The proposed project would be hosted at the University of Basel, under the supervision of Prof. Ernst Meyer and PD Dr. Thilo Glatzel, internationally renowned experts in atomic force microscopy (AFM), particularly non-contact AFM (nc-AFM), and surface physics. I'm very excited about the possibility of working with them and exploring a new research direction. As for the scientific idea itself, I'll keep the details under wraps until the project hopefully secures funding!

Encouraged by the positive evaluation, I revised the proposal based on the reviewers' comments and resubmitted it for the 2026 call. The feedback was valuable in strengthening the project, and I'm hopeful that the second attempt will be successful.

Fingers crossed for good news in 2027!
