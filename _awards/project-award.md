Research Grant from the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW)

March 2024 | Austria


I was very happy to receive a €75,000 research grant from the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW) through its 2023 Disruptive Innovation call, aimed at supporting promising ideas with the potential for scientific breakthroughs. The grant was awarded through a competitive selection process and represented the maximum funding available under the programme, supporting six months of research carried out in 2024.

As part of the selection process, shortlisted candidates were invited to present and defend their proposals through both a talk and a poster presentation at the Anton Zeilinger Salon at the Austrian Academy of Sciences. It was a great opportunity to discuss the proposed ideas and receive feedback from the scientific community.

My independent research project, "Detecting buried ELectronic states at heterostructures with a Variable gate-Electrode AFM" (***DELVE***), was a particularly exciting opportunity to explore some of my own scientific ideas and pursue a new research direction. Although six months is a rather short time for such an ambitious project, it was a great chance to test an unconventional approach to a challenging problem in surface physics.

The main goal of the project was to detect localized electronic states buried beneath the surface, which are typically inaccessible to even state-of-the-art surface-sensitive experimental techniques. The idea was to use the energy dissipated by an oscillating atomic force microscope cantilever as a probe of these hidden states. By electrically exciting subsurface defects and measuring the resulting energy loss through cantilever dissipation, we hoped to gain access to electronic properties that conventional surface measurements cannot easily reveal.

It was a challenging but exciting project, combining fundamental questions about the electronic structure of materials with the development of new experimental approaches. I'm grateful to the Austrian Academy of Sciences for the support and the opportunity to pursue this idea independently.
